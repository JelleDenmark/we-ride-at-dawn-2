# We Ride at Dawn 2: glossary

**Board / Lineup**: the rats you deploy, front first. `Lineup` is the snapshot type handed to the sim and stored in the league.
**Bench**: owned rats held back. They never fight.
**Rat**: a player unit. **Enemy**: a gauntlet unit. Both share one engine.
**Tier**: 1 to 3. Three copies of the same rat merge into the next tier.
**Raid**: the horde against the week's gauntlet, hourly. Pays scrap by depth. Never scores league points.
**Depth**: waves cleared in a raid. **Full clear**: clearing the whole gauntlet; earns a badge.
**Scrap**: the one currency. Spent in the shop on rats, relics, rerolls and slots.
**Duel**: one board against another (`simulateDuel`). Seat-fair and deterministic.
**Round**: the nightly 22:00 Copenhagen round robin. Night seed = the ride date. Each rat gets -1/0/+1 attack (`applyNightVariation`).
**League points**: 3 per win, 1 per draw, 0 per loss. Tiebreak is health margin.
**Season**: Monday 06:00 to Sunday 22:00 Copenhagen. Most league points wins.
**Open boards**: after each round every scored board is visible to everyone.
